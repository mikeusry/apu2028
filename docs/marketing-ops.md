# Marketing ops — Aug–Nov 2026

Locked 2026-08-16 after Perplexity research + verification. Our job is marketing. Hers is the work.

**Objective:** A coach who hears her name, sees her at an event, or clicks a follow can verify in under a minute that she is a real 2029 OF with speed, usable film, Stars National Walker, and a professional family-managed presence.

High-signal discoverability. Not more posts.

## Channel jobs

- **X** — coach-facing front door. Native 8–20s, one play. Identity line when it isn’t obvious: `2029 OF · Stars National Walker · Georgia`.
- **YouTube** — proof library. 90s–2½ min evaluation reel, game OF first. No weekly vlog.
- **Stars player page** — verification layer. Canonical softball URL. X bio and YouTube about must point here.
- **Email** — directed nudge. Subject: `Ayn Parker Usry | 2029 OF | Stars National Walker | [one actual update]`.

## Film order (any reel)

Best true-game OF play → another defensive play → speed/baserunning → offensive proof. Do not open with a title card, hype, lifting, or flag.

Email mini-reel: 30–60s, 4–6 distinct clips.

## Stop

Daily posting. Same play on every surface. Gym/lesson clips. Reading follows/likes as interest. Hashtag/media sprays. Mass email. Second website. Link-in-bio maze. Camps-for-content. Parent behavior that becomes the scouting report.

## NCAA — treat as a checklist, not a green light

D1 softball first recruiting contact is **September 1 of junior year**. For a 2029 that is commonly **September 1, 2027**. Confirm with current NCAA DI manual / Eligibility Center / a compliance office before any plan that assumes a coach can reply, call, or DM.

Social: NFCA 2018 guidance is “click, don’t type” (follow/like/share, no added words) before the contact date. Current bylaw text was not pulled in this session.

D3 / NAIA / JUCO often have no first-contact date. Still verify per school.
